When is the Causeway usually busiest?

  • Weekday mornings – heavy towards Singapore from Johor (workers/students), roughly 5–8 am.
  • Weekday evenings – heavy towards Johor on the way out and back into Singapore, roughly 5–9 pm.
  • Fridays, eves of public holidays, weekends & school holidays – longest queues both directions.
