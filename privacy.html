Airtitude Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Airtitude ("the app") shows weather, air quality, health trends, traffic and public transport information for Singapore and Malaysia. The app is published by Andrew Chan Poh Seng, Singapore.

Information we collect

We do not require an account and we do not collect your name, email address or contact details.

Location. If you allow it, the app uses your device's approximate location to show nearby weather, air quality and your position on the map. Your location is used on your device and when requesting local data from weather and map services. We do not store it on our servers or build a profile of you.

Destinations you type. If you use the "drive to" field, the text you enter is passed to Google Maps when you open directions. If you use the Travel tab, the places you enter are sent to our server to calculate routes.

Feedback. If you send feedback, the message you type is sent to us. Please do not include sensitive personal information.

Preferences. Settings such as read alerts are stored only on your device.

Third-party data sources

The app retrieves public data from the National Environment Agency and data.gov.sg (Singapore), the Land Transport Authority DataMall, Open-Meteo, NASA (FIRMS fire data and EONET), Frankfurter (exchange rates), Motorist.sg (Singapore pump prices), data.gov.my, Google Maps Platform and news RSS feeds. These services receive standard technical information such as your IP address when the app requests data, and handle it under their own privacy policies.

Advertising and tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs and no tracking across other apps or websites.

Data sharing and retention

We do not sell your data. We do not keep personal data beyond what is needed to respond to feedback you send.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Health information

Health trend information (such as influenza, HFMD and dengue case counts) is public data shown for general information only. It is not medical advice.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
